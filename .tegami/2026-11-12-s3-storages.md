### S3 storages for database models

`db.s3`, `db.extra_apps.<app>.s3` and `db.s3_storages` configure S3-compatible storages for `S3Field` columns from `tortoise-extensions`, with SSL toggled per storage. Setting `type: memory` or `type: local` swaps a storage for an in-memory or on-disk one for tests and local development. Extra apps without `s3` follow the top-level `db.s3`.
